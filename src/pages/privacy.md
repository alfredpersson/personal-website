# Privacy

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms and no analytics, sets no cookies and runs no JavaScript of its own.

One page, the RAG support assistant case study, embeds a video player from Tella. When you open that page, your browser loads the player from tella.tv, which applies its own privacy policy.

GitHub receives your IP address and browser information when you load a page, as any web host does. I do not have access to those logs. [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) explains how it handles them.

If you email me, I use your address and your message only to reply to you.
